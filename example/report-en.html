How it's calculated
Every situation in a round has a win probability: how many players are alive on each side and whether the bomb is planted (for example, 4v5 without a plant — about 30%). You killed an enemy — the team's odds went up, and this increase is credited to you; if a teammate damaged the enemy before, they get part of it (up to half — by damage share). You died — the odds dropped, which counts against you. The sum per round is your impact on the round, the average across rounds is the match rating. Odds come from the match database (regular players and pros) and are smoothed so that a rare situation doesn't give random numbers. Bomb plants and defuses aren't counted yet.

Glossary29 from this report. They're underlined with dots in the text — hover to see the explanation

ADR — Average damage per round (Average Damage per Round). Strong players have 80+.

KAST — % of rounds where the player got a kill, an assist, survived or was traded. Good — from 70%.

shoulder peek — Show your shoulder from cover and hide right away — bait a shot and find out if the angle is held.

info — Information about enemies: where they are, how many, where they're going. “Give info” — tell the team.

call — A short message to the team on voice: “two B”, “going”, “eco”.

counter-strafe — Pressing the opposite movement key before shooting to stop instantly: weapons are accurate only when still.

lurk — Playing alone away from the team: gathering info and hitting from behind.

molotov — Molotov/incendiary grenade: fire forces the enemy out of a position or blocks a path.

pop flash — A flash that pops right around the corner and blinds the enemy before they can turn away.

jiggle peek — “Jiggle peek” — a short peek out of cover and straight back: grab info or bait an AWP shot.

wide peek — “Wide peek” — a wide peek out of cover at full speed to see the whole area at once.

opening duel — The first kill (or first death) of the round decides who plays with the advantage.

off-angle — A non-standard position where you're not expected and not prefired.

peek — Stepping out of cover to see or shoot an enemy.

plant — Planting the bomb or the plant spot.

post-plant — The attackers' play after planting the bomb: holding positions around the bomb.

prefire — Shooting in advance at the spot where an enemy most likely stands, before you see them.

trade — Trade: a teammate immediately kills the one who killed you. A trade within 3–4 seconds is normal teamplay.

lineup — Grenade throws (smokes, flashes, molotovs) learned in advance for a specific situation.

smoke — Smoke grenade — blocks vision.

timing — The moment a player reaches a certain spot. “Catch on timing” — meet the enemy while they're moving.

tap — A single shot — for accuracy at long range.

team flash — A flash that blinded teammates.

force — Force buy: buying with all your money when you can't afford a full buy.

kill — Kill.

full buy — Full buy: armor, a rifle, grenades.

hold — Holding a position: you stand with your crosshair where the enemy will appear.

eco — Eco round: buy almost nothing to afford a full buy next round.

entry — The player who goes into the site first and takes the first duels.
